**Status:** validated research portfolio result.

Combining P1/P4/P5/P6/P7/P8/P9 created an unrestricted research ceiling near:

- +$170,274 Jan-Jul
- ~557,050 trades
- 7/7 positive months.

The important breakthrough was not the unrestricted total. It was the portfolio-cap curve:

- max 2 sleeves: ~+$106.7K / 334K trades
- max 3: ~+$141.1K / 451K
- **max 4: ~+$161.3K / 522K**
- max 5: ~+$168.7K / 551K
- unlimited: ~+$170.3K / 557K

Four concurrent sleeves captured roughly 95% of unrestricted net without unlimited stacking.

## Rule carried forward

Concurrency count alone is not the final risk variable. Same-direction stacking can remain productive; later research showed that **opposite-direction conflict** is the more important risk state.

Portfolio evaluation must therefore be chronological and account-level. Never sum isolated sleeve backtests as if they were simultaneously executable.
