# 03 — P4 Rotation / P6 Expansion Specialists

**Status:** validated research specialists; not independently MT5-certified.

## P4 — Rotation

State:
- low/normal volatility;
- statistically extreme displacement;
- inefficient travel / weak directional persistence.

Policy:
- FADE the extreme;
- wide emergency room;
- early favorable-excursion ignition;
- tight high-water harvest.

Representative Jan-Jul result:
- net ≈ +$19,457
- trades ≈ 104,397
- gross loss ≈ -$41,443
- PF ≈ 1.47
- realized DD ≈ $76

## P6 — Expansion

State:
- same family of extreme events,
- but high volatility and/or high directional efficiency.

Policy:
- CONTINUE displacement rather than fade it.

Representative Jan-Jul:
- net ≈ +$16,477
- trades ≈ 77,327
- gross loss ≈ -$37,729
- 7/7 positive months
- rolling May/Jun/Jul remained positive.

## Architectural breakthrough

Rotation and continuation are **complementary policies for different trajectory states**, not rival explanations of the same move.

Cost-aware meta-labeling is required. Transaction costs must be embedded in the label / action-value calculation rather than subtracted as an afterthought.
