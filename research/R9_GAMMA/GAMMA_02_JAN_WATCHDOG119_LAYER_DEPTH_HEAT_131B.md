## Scientific interpretation

1. **Realized gross loss / balance DD** is strongly associated with deeper renewal layers and can be reduced causally with a maximum layer rule.
2. **Floating equity DD** is dominated by synchronized concurrent inventory. A layer rule alone cannot solve this while preserving high-capture cap703+ economics.

This means the path to a reliable `$200K+` January architecture is **not simply more capacity**. We need more profit per unit of simultaneous inventory and/or complementary non-overlapping specialists.

The best next hypotheses are:

- causal pre-admission inventory-shape/ownership control that prevents correlated bursts rather than reacting after drawdown appears;
- HARVEST→RUNNER promotion for a carefully selected subset so persistent movement earns more per occupied slot;
- complementary W01-W04 session/state specialists that generate profit when Watchdog is inactive instead of stacking more W05 tickets;
- arbitrary-start and capital overlays after the January architecture has enough week/state coverage.

Generic hard stops, forced child timeouts, aggregate floating-loss gates and naive same-tick multiplicity caps remain rejected from 131A unless a materially different mechanism is introduced.
