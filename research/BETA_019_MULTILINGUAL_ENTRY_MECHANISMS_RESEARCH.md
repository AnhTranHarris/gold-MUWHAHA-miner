## 2. Eight independent source-reconstructible entry experiments (proposed, not passed)

### E1: event-time quote-imbalance ignition (genuinely new to BETA)
Input: ordered Dukascopy Bid/Ask quote ticks, midpoint m=(Bid+Ask)/2; NEVER infer actual buyer/seller executed order volume from quote updates. At tick i compute u/d signs from the previous observed midpoint and keep a causal ring of the last N non-flat changes (N hypothesis grid 16/32/64). Flow F=(up-down)/(up+down), absolute signed displacement D=m_i-m_(i-N), normalized efficiency E=abs(D)/sum_abs_quote_changes. Close optional fixed-N quote bars or signed-imbalance bars at the CURRENT tick boundary using EWMA expected count/imbalance from already completed events only. Permit continuation long when F>0 and D>0 and price-relative 1s/5s ignition direction agrees; symmetric short. Alternative early reversal: flow crosses zero AFTER a frozen sweep/reclaim. Initial grid N=16,32,64 and F threshold .2/.4/.6; calculate 1s/5s pending-time freshness. Critical: ensure high activity generates many candidates rather than static M5 trend filter vetoing them, and deduplicate repeated reports from one move by setup/event ID.
Success hypothesis: this event-time microstructure timing could rescue opportunities between fixed S1 and M1 candles while quote traffic rises. Failure risks: feed-specific repeated quotes/bid-ask bounce, count inflation from correlated duplicate ticks, latency, and overfit EWMA parameters. For quote-only source call metric QUOTE_SIGN_IMBALANCE, never VPIN, actual aggressive buys, or genuine trade delta. Source MQL5 Parts 38 and Beyond Clock Part 1.

### E2: frozen level -> first sweep -> bounded reclaim -> renewed impulse
Construct confirmed M15/M5 highs/lows with left=2,right=2 fully CLOSED right-hand bars before level activation. Record (level price, confirmation timestamp, side, ATR14 from closed M5, consumed flag). Level ARMED -> one observed Bid/Ask breach of depth at least 0.1*ATR14 (screen 0.1/0.2/0.3) -> tick or next completed M1 close/recover inside -> optional 250ms/1s quote-flow reacceleration opposite breach -> execute only at current-or-later real executable ask/bid. Expire after at most 1/2/3 closed M1 bars or if sweep extreme invalidated; permit first interaction once per structural level. Independently evaluate fast-tick reclaim versus stricter fully closed-M1 reclaim, so waiting too long is measurable. Label each as hypothesis, not 'real stop-hunting evidence'.
Sources MQL5 confirmed fractals, Chinese BoS, TradingView Pineify one-shot ATR frozen sweeps. Compare to simple reversal without sweep and to BETA001 and to E1 no-structure alternative. Important: do not attribute pivot to original center bar until right-hand candles exist.

### E3: confirmed break -> retest -> second ignition
Setup: last confirmed M5/M15 swing / session box; breakout requires a CLOSED M1 body beyond frozen structural level (or compare separate intratick fast-acceptance branch). Freeze broken level. After at least one subsequent observed quote retraces to level within δ={0.1,0.2,0.3}*closed ATR_M5, require renewed 1s or 5s outward displacement and entry at next executable quote; cancel on closed M1 reclaim inside box, spread deterioration, or 2 M1 timeouts.
