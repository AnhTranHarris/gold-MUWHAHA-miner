## Policy pivot and scientific definition
- **ENTRY, HOLD and EXIT receive EQUAL default importance and parallel research attention (1/3, 1/3, 1/3).** This is a research allocation, not a promise that an arithmetic weighted loss is actual after-cost profit. Specialists may reweight internal entry/hold/exit behavior by **CAUSALLY observed** regime, structural event, quote path, time-in-state, volatility, spread and current position; record exact weights, state transition times, and rule code. Weights do not depend on future MFE/MAE or SYNTH/OVERFIT oracle.
- **Trade outcome:** A position is a realized loss if its actual valid close after spread/fees is negative; an initially negative or temporarily unfavorable +3/+5/+15 second quote markout does not *alone* label the original entry “bad.” Keep unrealized MAE, stop-outs, available liquidity, margin, missed rearm, equity drawdown, and opportunity-cost as essential diagnostics; even a final winning trade can have large adverse exposure or block better trades.
- **Realized net profit, expectancy, profit factor, gross loss, max equity drawdown, loss-tail, net/trade and absolute # of profitable trades are primary combined-system metrics.** Fixed-horizon quote markouts and R9 SYNTH time/side recall stay independent micro-entry diagnostic columns, not substitute for realized economics.
- The candidate *must be modular*: `MarketState` (current event-regime), `StructureState` (causally confirmed levels, sweep/accept/retest), `ExecutionState` (real observed tick quote, spread, cost, latency), `EntrySpecialist`, `HoldPolicy`, `ExitPolicy`, `RiskGate`, `PortfolioArbiter`. Each has deterministic as-of snapshot fields, rule ID, input data index, and tick visibility contract.
- Portfolio arbitration is ONE account, ONE open 0.01 lot position. The four specialist experiments may execute as parallel OFFLINE RESEARCH JOBS against independent *copies* of the same research source; do not count 3–5 independent profitable ledgers as one combined account or assume simultaneous funded execution.
