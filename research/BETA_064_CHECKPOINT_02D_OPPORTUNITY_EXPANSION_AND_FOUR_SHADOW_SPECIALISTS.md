## Objective

Continue increasing real Entry→Hold opportunity count while preserving the owner's >=85% monthly survivability requirement, and investigate up to four genuinely new Entry specialist families without polluting BETA with dead Alpha/GAMMA logic.

The new specialists are introduced through a **shadow-desk quarantine**:

1. generate causal proposals;
2. label on Dukascopy raw tick chronology;
3. measure standalone survivability and favorable-first-passage behavior;
4. search only reconstructible causal substate filters;
5. allow no production ownership until a desk earns probationary status;
6. frozen Major Checkpoint 02 always remains the control.

## Public-mechanics research basis

The research used reconstructible mechanics only, not community performance claims.

Relevant recurring public concepts:
- locked session high/low and wick sweep + close back inside;
- London / New York session-specific treatment rather than one universal daily rule;
- sweep followed by market-structure shift / displacement confirmation;
- session VWAP/value plus liquidity sweep mean-reversion;
- opening-range breakout/retest;
- compression before expansion;
- overlap-specific friction/spread controls;
- tick-volume / activity confirmation on breakout or false-break behavior.

These mechanics are treated as hypotheses only and are independently tested on the BETA Dukascopy corpus.

## Opportunity architecture recommendation

Future BETA opportunity discovery should use three layers:

### 1. Frozen executable desks
The current Major Checkpoint 02 E1–E12 + six-session system.

### 2. Probationary gap-fill desks
A new specialist may trade only in chronological gaps left by the frozen/accepted child architecture and only after independent Entry→Hold quality tests.

### 3. Shadow opportunity bus
Unproven specialists generate timestamped proposals and state packets but **cannot own a position**.

This lets BETA search a much wider state space without weakening the production-quality Entry→Hold gate.

## Four new shadow specialists evaluated

### E13 — Absorption-Divergence Reversal

Mechanism:
- candidate is near a confirmed prior 15-minute extreme;
- quote activity is elevated;
- signed quote-pressure / quote-volume behavior diverges from the price push;
- reversal is not heavily contaminated by adverse higher-timeframe ownership;
- execution friction must be low;
- short-horizon path efficiency must confirm that the rejection has begun.

This is the only new specialist that reached probationary quality in this unit.

A compact reconstructible child rule:

- friction <= 0.09
- tick_z >= 1.00
- eff60 >= 0.50
- r900 × side >= -0.50 × atr300

Raw Jan–Jul candidate result:
- 40 proposals
- 85.00% Entry→Hold survivability
- 37.50% favorable-first-passage success
- +$21.936 diagnostic path-resolution value

When allowed to fill only gaps not occupied by the Checkpoint-02C child:
- 10 incremental trades
- 100% observed Entry→Hold survivability
- 40% favorable-first-passage success
- +$10.298 diagnostic path-resolution value
- observed activity in Jan–May; no qualifying idle additions Jun–Jul

Checkpoint-02C + probationary E13:
- 5,529 trades
- 88.23% weighted survivability
- 60.73% weighted favorable-first-passage success
- +$6,022.475 diagnostic path-resolution value
- every month remains >85% survivability

Scientific caveat:
the compact E13 rule was selected after Jan–Jul inspection. Leave-one-month-out rule selection does not reproduce an >=85% standalone held-out result in every month. Therefore E13 is **PROBATIONARY SHADOW/GAP-FILL ONLY**, not a frozen production specialist.

## February / Checkpoint-02C robustness refinement

A simple universal threshold increase was tested as a potential fix for the slight February leave-one-month-out miss.

Example:
- raise non-E6/non-E11 authority from 0.9145 to 0.916
- observed child shrinks from 5,519 to ~5,329 trades
- February observed survivability rises to ~86.46%
- weighted survivability rises to ~88.52%

However, session×specialist SYNTH condition-cell coverage falls to approximately **4.19%**, below the frozen Major Checkpoint-02 4.24% control.

Decision:
**REJECT universal tightening as the primary solution.**

It repairs the quality statistic mainly by deleting under-covered opportunities and therefore works against the opportunity-recovery objective.

The February problem should instead be solved through state discrimination within weak E6/E7/E9 cells.
