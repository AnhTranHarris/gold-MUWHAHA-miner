# VNext C4 — Chronologically purged broker×month competing-path and quoted-net meta-admission

Research provenance: Original source exact raw DUKAS/MT5 Jan/Feb/Jun/Jul archived 8/8 SHA, R24 original exact first crossing engine SHA-pinned and original C3 exact 8 source-month/3 cycle selected-trade ordinal traces saved in MT5_EXPAND. Upstream source and the MQL5 EA are not changed.

The user requires high *correct* trade volume per original R9 SYNTH source-UTC 15-minute, hour and day for both brokers. C1 structural reclaim, C2 bid/ask impulsive mark concordance and C3 HTF/flow adaptive multi-horizon had 24 full source-month original quote replays, **all 24 negative modeled quote-price expectancy and zero active hours jointly meeting ≥80% target-first correctness plus exact R9 hourly quota**. Thus a new family must *reject* erroneous candidates in addition to generating more trades.

**C4 new architectural hypothesis:** Conditional, broker-conditioned competing first-event hazard plus conditional expected quoted-liquidation-value meta-classification can improve C3's realized correct action fraction by avoiding fragile source quote regimes while maintaining some volume. C4 computes exactly the original 23 prefix-only source quote features at original executed C3 signal quote ordinals, plus original *as-of* decision side, intended maximum holding H, source spread-normalized stop and TP, and prior source flow score. It fits an explicit three-class LightGBM on the first nine original UTC calendar days of EACH broker-source-month: {STOP_FIRST, TARGET_FIRST, TIMEOUT}, and a separate robust regression to observed quoted-price net after bid/ask exit. Future outcomes are labels only; neither quota nor future-selected teacher side nor realized exits enter model features. Days 10 and 20 are complete purges, days 11–19 are the unchanged calibration diagnostic, and days 21–month end are a later-day test not used to change the pretrained gates. The same four model gates are frozen for all 8 source months, each independent broker/month: `P(TARGET)>=.55`, `>=.65`, `>=.75`, and `P(TARGET)>=.55 AND predicted_net_raw>0`.

**Risk control and scientific boundaries:** C4 selects a SUBSET of the previously executed C3 chronological one-position positions. It deliberately never invents trades during released idle time, so it is a conservative subset study, not a full rerouted/broker-restream simulator. Every selected position preserves original exact signal/fill/exit ordinals and original quoted-price net; unselected positions are WAIT, not wins. Trace original source SHA required. All 120 day and 2880 hour / 11520 quarter calendar rows per source are retained for each gate, but C4 test scores must be evaluated **only** on days ≥21 because the first nine source days were used for fitting; a whole-month R9 quota pass is not claimed from a partial-month gate. No blind OOS: all four original months have influenced earlier research. This is intended to diagnose *separability*, not certify deployability.

**Falsification and full recorded negative results:** Full 8 broker×month source model binaries and all original per-position C3 signal ordinal score+class+quoted-net arrays are preserved in the C4 archive. Across eight historical months and 32 fixed meta gate variants, ZERO original active test hours meet both >=80% target-first correctness and original R9 hourly trade count. Most tighter gates select zero. Source-specific later-day ROC-AUC by month: DUKAS Jan .513, Feb .534, Jun .544, Jul .529; MT5 Jan .517, Feb .540, Jun .539, Jul .555. These later-day AUC values are near random even though in-sample training AUC is higher (.585–.729). Thus the particular C3 source-action labels cannot be reliably predicted by this short-history 23-feature 3-cause meta head across later regimes. The occasional tiny gate has negative quoted-price net; no profitability. There is no route to high correct-trade volume by simply imposing >65% model posterior cutoffs.

**New next-cycle design (not yet executed):** Abandon the same preselected market-entry score-only family. Test independently **observed two-stage quote-event activation**: anchor a qualifying 15m/1h trend/macro structure from completed past quotes, then wait for a *real* source-ordinal pullback into an as-of estimate of liquidity imbalance, then a second source-observed reclaim impulse before sending a dealer-style aggressive order at actual next quote; allow CANCEL/WAIT if spread broadens; explicitly compensate DUKAS source high spread with price discovery rather than unverified passive limit fills. Distinct layer: competing risks for adverse quote gap, microstructure state transitions and occupancy opportunity cost, and endogenous exit/trailing policy; no full60s stop-free hold where strict hourly quota cannot be met under one-position theorem. Multi-broker source-specific geometry/horizon/session must be trained on a split with purged no-lookahead and then tested after training freeze; source costs, dealer fills and future official calendar remain UNKNOWN. Do not repeat C1–C4 negative parameter grids unchanged.
