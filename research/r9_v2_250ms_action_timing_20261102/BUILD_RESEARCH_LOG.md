## B. New hypothesis and previously missing actions

Unlike every prior model, a decision timestamp is now generated by EVERY eligible causal 250ms quote-slot event after the initial 300s full local-clock warmup and an observed >=$0.02 midpoint change. No reliance on the old baseline entry timestamps or old position-held restrictions. For EACH event, create eight teacher-only competing actions:

1. BUY NOW (quote at decision tick)
2. SELL NOW
3. BUY at earliest future executable quote >=250ms after decision
4. SELL at +250ms
5. BUY at earliest quote >=1000ms
6. SELL at +1000ms
7. BUY at earliest quote >=3000ms
8. SELL at +3000ms

A WAIT/DELAY runtime action is a commitment to **reevaluate** on the later quote with then-current information. It may NOT know the future-best side or future-best delay when deciding now. Future oracle maxima are not runtime decisions.

### Strict mutually exclusive training labels

From the point of hypothetical execution, examine source bid/ask quotes IN THEIR ORIGINAL FILE SEQUENCE, including later records that share the same millisecond timestamp. For BUY exit at Bid; SELL exit at Ask. Initial hard stop R9-compatible 0.30 from entry-side quote; trailing activates at gross +0.10 with 0.03 trailing gap and `round(floor(v/.01+.5)*.01,2)` stop rounding; stop or first quote at/after 30s closes. Fixed research round trip fee $0.02; no claimed live slippage/margin/partial fill.

The first positive barrier is executable NET >= +0.10 (after fee); the adverse frontier is the liquidation-side quote moving >= $0.10 AGAINST its value at entry. The +0.10 adverse frontier is a scientific diagnostic, NOT the actual 0.30 broker hard stop. Strict CORRECT requires favorable barrier FIRST, positive realized R9 exit, and closure <=30,000ms from entry. INCORRECT requires first adverse frontier or nonpositive close when a frontier is observed. NEUTRAL means neither initial frontier reached, or censored data / timing ambiguity; neutral is never collapsed into incorrect or treated as correct. A quote arriving after a long weekend is not a 30-second success.

The teacher labels all eight actions using *later* ticks offline. Foresight features are generated exclusively from tick indices <= each NOW entry event. The newly trained WAIT-value target is side-specific: `NOW not CORRECT` AND `some delayed action at SAME SIDE CORRECT`. No selecting a future direction ex post. A consequence remains: prospective WAIT ranking does NOT by itself prove that a later online policy can pick the exact profitable quote.
