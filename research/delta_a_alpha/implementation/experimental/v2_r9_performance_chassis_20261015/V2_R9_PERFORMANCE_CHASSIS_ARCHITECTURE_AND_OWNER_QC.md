## 1. The architecture error and its correction

The earlier experiments optimized an S15 squeeze runner, individual high-ATR trailing, M1 second-phase vetoes, or generic reversal/flow indicators under isolated capital simulations. Although many were causal research experiments, they did not test the completed **original cumulative V1 G0 → session → completed HTF → L3 → L4 → L5 → L6 → physical L7** program as one system. Conversely, the current V1 executable `FourSessionV1` imposes its existing session-specific L2 source permissions and per-desk multi-minute holding exits on candidate proposals. Merely injecting a 20-second R9 or squeeze proposal at G0 does not install R9's native **minute-frozen bracket / completed S1 efficiency / conditional same-minute opposite rearm / 30-second position lifecycle**. Those differences change signal admission, slot occupancy, trade throughput, gross loss and hourly cash.

**Correction:** Do not optimize another low-velocity component and then add percentages. Build a second **research chassis** in which inherited original-native session grids, R9-derived microcycles and prospective specialists are *virtual and competing at the same tick-rooted event boundary* and are admitted through one account-level funded queue. Keep the old current V1 full-source executable and whitepaper untouched as the fallback. This is a future-version architectural experiment allowed by the V1 whitepaper's version contract; the owner must explicitly accept it to change the official spine.

```
L0: Immutable ordered original Bid/Ask quote, UTC milliseconds, symbol economics
     ↓

     ↓

     ↓
L3: Native-H4/H1 + native-M1/M5 lattice + tick/S1/R9 intraminute lattice
    (virtual proposals, each tagged with first available timestamp, quote and ancestry)
     ↓
L4: Causal regime/auction activity and Watchdog scouted vs actually funded renewals
     ↓
L5: Trend-within-trend *role router*: trend / pullback / opposed-transition / range;
    prevent naive 'H1 and M5 signs must agree' admission collapse
     ↓
L6: Fixed-size, bounded, failure-evidenced recovery and lifecycle selection;
    conditional M5 ATR profit-harvest exits are candidate policies, never proven profits
     ↓
L7: One account ledger: margin reserve, source-priority, risk heat,
    one position maximum for protected $100 research, 250ms delayed actual quote,
    opposing Bid/Ask close, spread, commission, trailing and marked-equity DD
     ↓
Immutable source-owned paid close → update legitimate renewal/rearm → new proposals
```

**Not acceptable:** multi-grid virtual sources accumulating their own invented physical profits; a fixed completed-H1/M5 alignment veto that discards the historically important transition cohort without evidence; backtesting only a standalone child while naming it 'full V1'; optimization of a candidate's stop distance while the admission and funded lifecycle remain incompatible.
