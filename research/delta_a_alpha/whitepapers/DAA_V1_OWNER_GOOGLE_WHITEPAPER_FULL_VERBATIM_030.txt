1. EXECUTIVE DECISION


Vertical Grid System V1 is the permanent architectural spine for Delta-A-alpha.


The system must preserve this order:


ordered ticks

This sequence is not a temporary January or February experiment. It is the V1 system contract.


Future versions V2, V3, and later may refine geometry, participation intensity, state definitions, lifecycle rules, recovery rules, specialist selection, heat allocation, and small-capital survivability. They may not casually bypass, flatten, or replace the spine. A future version that changes the architecture must be explicitly promoted by the owner as a new spine version after regression testing.


2. WHY V1 EXISTS


The high-performing January and February research showed that XAUUSD cannot be treated as one homogeneous grid process.


The strongest results came from preserving a layered machine:


• the ordered tick path defines what actually happened;
• session state determines the market prior and the appropriate grid geometry;
• completed higher-timeframe structure determines structural permission;
• high-volume London/overlap/New York periods require dense hourly harvesting mechanics;
• Asia requires a different geometry and participation model;
• renewal/Watchdog logic captures persistent high-quality campaigns;
• trend-within-trend routing chooses the appropriate continuation or pullback specialist;
• wrong-direction recovery is conditional and state-owned rather than generic reversal;
• a global capital/heat governor controls physical exposure across all layers.


The research failure mode to avoid is compressing these layers into a bag of independent specialist streams. The layers form one vertical system.


3. V1 HARD DESIGN ORDER


LAYER 0 — ORDERED TICK EXECUTION ROOT


Exact ordered Bid/Ask ticks are authoritative for:
• event identity;
• first touch/crossing;
• fills;
• stops;
• trailing behavior;
• reclaim/retest timing;
• renewal timing;
• wrong-direction failure timing;
• position lifecycle;
• equity/heat replay.


Derived bars may provide context but may not replace the tick execution path.


No future tick, unfinished bar, retrospective event label, future MFE/MAE, or future P/L may influence a live decision.


LAYER 1 — SESSION-SPECIFIC GRID GEOMETRY


Session state is a first-class system variable, not a reporting label.


The system must support distinct market geometry for at least:
• Asia;
• London;
• London/New York overlap;
• New York;
• late New York / transition where evidence supports it.


The system must not assume one universal gap, cadence, TP/SL, holding horizon, or participation rate across all sessions.


Asia and London/NY are explicitly different families.


Asia:
• quieter/rotational and state-selective geometry;
• lower opportunity density in many regimes;
• activity/subphase filters when justified;
• separate continuation/reversion semantics;
• no forced London/NY cadence.


London/overlap/New York:
• high-volume opportunity harvesting;
• hour/subphase-specific cadence;
• denser causal event manufacture only after structural permission;
• explicit capacity allocation because low-value dense sources can consume slots needed by higher-value sources.


Calendar month is never a deployable execution feature.


LAYER 2 — COMPLETED MULTI-TIMEFRAME STRUCTURE


The canonical structural stack is completed:
H4 → H1 → M15 → M5


The system does not use naive timeframe voting.


Role separation:


H4:
environment and long-horizon directional/volatility context.


H1:
structural location and parent directional structure.


M15:
phase, pullback, continuation, transition, or local structural state.


M5:
transfer/opportunity state immediately above the tick layer.


Ticks:
event identity, execution, micro-timing, and path ownership.


Future versions may add D1, M1, subsecond bars, or additional derived context, but they must preserve role separation and tick-rooted execution.


LAYER 3 — HOURLY HIGH-VOLUME HARVESTING


London, overlap, and New York contain distinct hourly auction phases.


V1 therefore permits different:
• event cadence;
• grid spacing;
• displacement requirement;
• lifecycle;
• cap;
• source-slot priority


by causal hour/subphase and completed structural state.


The January work demonstrated that broad session averages hide hour-specific opportunity. The February rebuild confirmed that the high-volume hourly layer must remain part of the primary spine rather than be demoted to an optional specialist.


One physical ticket per market tick is the default supplemental opportunity rule unless an explicitly declared grid-scaling mechanism owns the multiplicity.


LAYER 4 — WATCHDOG / REGIME RENEWAL


Watchdog is a causal renewal/campaign layer.


Core semantics:
• first-parent or scout behavior;
• earned admission from already-realized outcomes;
• renewal child geometry;
• relock after poor/slow realized renewal;
• maximum renewal-depth control;
• explicit campaign ownership;
• global concurrency interaction.


Watchdog capacity is state dependent. January may justify deeper renewal inventory; February showed that once other layers become stronger, shallower Watchdog inventory can free physical capacity for higher-value opportunities.


The system must optimize total portfolio economics, not maximize Watchdog ticket count in isolation.


LAYER 5 — TREND-WITHIN-TREND NATIVE ROUTING


The system may operate multiple continuation/pullback/phase specialists, but routing must be based on entry-known state.


The router may use:
• completed H4/H1/M15/M5 state;
• direction;
• realized favorable displacement;
• session/hour/subphase;
• activity regime;
• state age;
• causal renewal behavior;
• already-realized specialist outcomes where an online confidence layer is explicitly used.


The router may not use:
• month name;
• date identity;
• future outcome;
• hindsight regime classification.


The purpose is to allow a lower-timeframe scalping process to trade inside a higher-timeframe structural context without collapsing all timeframes into one vote.


LAYER 6 — WRONG-DIRECTION RECOVERY


Recovery is part of the permanent spine, but generic reversal is forbidden.


A wrong-direction recovery may activate only after a causally defined failure state is observed.


Examples of admissible evidence:
• failed ignition within a fixed already-elapsed window;
• failure to reach an expected continuation quantum;
• structural reclaim after failed break;
• state-conditioned reversal evidence;
• already-observed loss of continuation quality.


Recovery must:
• remain fixed 0.01 lot under the current research policy;
• never increase size because the first trade lost;
• never become Martingale;
• never become DCA rescue;
• have its own lifecycle, cap, and invalidation;
• be independently measurable.


February proved that a generic March-style reversal is not portable. Recovery must be owned by session/state geometry.


LAYER 7 — PORTFOLIO HEAT / CAPITAL GOVERNOR


All physical sleeves share one account-level governor.


The governor owns:
• maximum simultaneous exposure;
• per-layer/per-session capacity;
• physical slot allocation;
• reduce-only behavior;
• collision/de-duplication;
• account-level drawdown/heat controls;
• future small-account survivability mechanics.


The governor may reduce physical exposure without deleting the shadow/intelligence layer.


The system must distinguish:
• signal intelligence;
• physical execution;
• capital capacity.


A strategy may remain active in shadow while receiving zero funded slots.
