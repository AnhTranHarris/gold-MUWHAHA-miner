# GRID-001 January Dual Proof Ownership 001

**Status:** COMPLETE / NEGATIVE MR OWNERSHIP

Continuation and mean-reversion were allowed to race for a frozen +0.25 event-gap proof before any physical trade opened. The first side to prove itself received one 0.01 trade with ±1 event-gap TP/SL inside the original five-minute horizon.

Result: mean-reversion ownership was consistently inferior. A15 continuation-owned events were mildly positive overall (**+$188.18 / PF 1.0155**) and strong in validation (**+$851.85 / PF 1.0943**), while A15 mean-reversion-owned events lost **-$1,408.57 / PF 0.8801**. Combining both directions produced **-$1,220.39 / PF 0.9489**.

Therefore the first opposite-side proof is not a valid mean-reversion entry signal. It may still be useful as a veto or state-change signal.

**Decision:** reject dual-direction ownership, retain continuation proof and possible opposite-proof veto, and move to a larger structural direction mechanism rather than tuning proof distance.
