## Risk, survivability, and scientific decision

**No tested combination meets all owner constraints.** Largest one-quote loss around **−$125 per ounce equivalent** remains possible under the sampled exact source quotes; a 0.01 lot XAUUSD contract may represent about one ounce for brokers specifying 100 oz per full lot, but broker Coinexx symbol specification, USD margin, commissions, latency, stop order slippage, and physical account DD were NOT observed. Thus no $100/$200/$300 account survivability or R9 SYNTH trade-velocity parity can be certified. R9 SYNTH is a historical comparator, never a side oracle. Results depend on already-studied Jan-Jul, multiple testing and unrealistically optimistic same-quote T2 execution; source-native entry does **not** equal a real Coinexx fill.

**Useful scientific leads**: (1) Watchdog economic *maturity* can reallocate slots and increase historical weak-hour support, but must be paired with loss/heat-aware admission. (2) H4/M15/M5 scouts raise gross opportunity supply, but require source-cost and loss-budget veto/priority; H4 direction is not a guarantee of trend continuation. (3) An L6 mid-trade source-quote warning/reduce-only mechanism can change loss distribution, but automatic reversal worsened it; combine L6 with truthful L7 balance/equity and broker-aware margin BEFORE any performance claim. (4) Watchdog shadow economic marks are not FUNDED already-realized outcomes; real L4 needs account outcome records.
