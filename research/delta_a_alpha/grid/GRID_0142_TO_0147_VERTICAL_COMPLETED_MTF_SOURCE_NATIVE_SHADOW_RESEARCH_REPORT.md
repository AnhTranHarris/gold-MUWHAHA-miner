Nevertheless, a profitable, high-density, low-risk **hourly portfolio** has **not** been demonstrated. Most original weak hours remain uncovered and source quote loss tails are too large. Even the best source quote day-cluster confidence intervals include zero, and all months were previously researched.

Next GRID0148 should preregister a bounded **online source-distribution / execution-risk and entry-ownership** mechanism before testing, rather than adding another hindsight narrow time window. Distinguish actual qualified daily trades from virtual signals.
