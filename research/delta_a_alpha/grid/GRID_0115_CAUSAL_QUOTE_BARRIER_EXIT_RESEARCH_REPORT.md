## Different hypothesis, honestly tested

On ORIGINAL causal grid quotes, do asymmetric time-ordered exit boundaries change expectancy? Fixed 3 entry cohorts (REF_T1; source-observed FALSE_BREAK_R2; and first-election race FALSE_BREAK versus ACCEPT_DEEP). For each choose 3 profit-taking marks (+$1,+$2,+$4) crossed with 3 adverse marks (−$1,−$2,−$4), actual signed after-source-Bid/Ask quote-side and $0.02 assumed 1oz research fee; first quote reach, otherwise 600-second timeout actual source quote. Nine risk geometries ×3 entry cohorts ×7 months = **189 strategy-geometry-month screens**. Triggered quote may overshoot threshold: actual opposite-side exit quote not fictional barrier price. No full portfolio, MT5 fill, margin, slippage, overlap or account profit claims.

## Outcome

**All 27 risk/entry combinations were negative for pooled Jan–Jul 600s first-passage outcomes; every variant's monthly mean remained negative in all seven months.** The least negative stop-target geometry was approximately **−$0.709** average quote-side per original source signal (FALSE_BREAK_R2 with +$4 target / −$4 stop). That mask supports only about 750/1389 historically weak UTC hours and is not an actionable profitable strategy. Unfiltered T1 baseline with +$1/−$1 exits averages −$0.799 and supports 1361/1389 weak hours. Adding time-ordered exits and stop-target convexity does not repair the underlying economics; avoiding overfitting by choosing the best cell after 27 tests is imperative.

Some unusual exit-stop counts and nonzero overlap can reflect market high-frequency quote chronology but do not certify brokerage execution. Quote-side overshoot and $0.02 fee included; +$0.10 and +$0.25 hypothetical roundtrip additional costs can only make these negative results worse. Latency models were preregistered for any apparently positive candidate; none was positive, so no latency-success evidence exists. Original 75% of same-day R9 SYNTH qualified executable opportunities never established by these quote-candidate counts. Full per-month/UTC-day/UTC-hour, every barrier combination including zero-support hours saved, plus deterministic first-passage tests.

## Why test this and next question

MetaQuotes and community breakout/retest systems use staged price-state machines; none proves positive XAUUSD profitability on the original Dukascopy source. A causal stop-target can reduce loss tails or alter winner fraction but **cannot create directional forecasting edge** when source quote friction dominates realized movement. Before testing more high-dimensional signal combinations, decompose average source-spread cost vs signed midpoint displacement on each original poor-hour segment using exact algebraic identity. This becomes preregistered GRID0116 and explicitly keeps all prior results.
