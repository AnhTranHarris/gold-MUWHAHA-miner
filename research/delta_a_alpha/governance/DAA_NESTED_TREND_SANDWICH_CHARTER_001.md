## New architectural assumption — trend within trend

Every timeframe/horizon may contain its own local trend/state.

Examples:
- 5-second uptrend inside a 1-minute pullback;
- 1-minute pullback inside a 15-minute uptrend;
- 15-minute trend inside an H1 range.

Higher timeframe context is therefore **not a binary veto** and no majority-vote design is assumed.

Each scale should provide:
- local direction;
- trend/range strength;
- volatility state;
- phase/age;
- ownership/authority over the event horizon.

Cross-scale disagreement is information.

## Multi-state market awareness

Each scale may eventually carry a compact state such as:

`{direction, efficiency/chop, volatility, phase, transition}`

- UP / DOWN / RANGE;
- QUIET / NORMAL / EXPANSION / SHOCK;
- EARLY / MATURE / EXHAUSTING / TRANSITION.

The first category attack must stay narrow and causal.
