# Delta-A-alpha — January Hyper-Fast Discovery & Lattice Research Charter

**Unit:** `DAA_JANUARY_HYPERFAST_DISCOVERY_LATTICE_CHARTER_001`  
**Status:** GOVERNING RESEARCH CHARTER  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose

Establish a hyper-fast causal research battleground for the R9 Delta-A-alpha grid system without turning discovery into endless fine-tuning.

January 2026 Dukascopy XAUUSD ordered ticks are the primary invention laboratory.

The objective is to discover structural mechanisms large enough to materially improve the system, especially gross loss and drawdown, while preserving the opportunity density needed to help bridge the R9 REAL -> R9 SYNTH gap.

## January role

January is the discovery battlefield.

Use January to:
- hypothesize aggressively;
- reconstruct public mechanisms;
- create original mechanism mutations and combinations;
- falsify weak ideas quickly;
- compare architectural alternatives;
- discover large causal improvements;
- identify interactions worth carrying forward.

Do not spend the research budget polishing tiny threshold gains while the architecture remains structurally weak.

Parameter exploration is allowed when it tests a mechanism's neighborhood or identifies a robust knee. It must not become an endless single-point optimizer.

## Jan-Jul role

January-only success is not promotion.

Once a sufficiently meaningful candidate architecture exists:
1. freeze the candidate family;
2. replay January through July month by month;
3. diagnose which months and states fail;
4. correct the mechanism without destroying what already works;
5. build the next refinement vertically on the last valid version;
6. repeat monthly diagnostics;
7. preserve exact rollback checkpoints.

The objective is cumulative system construction, not month-specific replacement.

August 2026 remains SEALED.

## Current system role

The R9 Delta-A-alpha grid is a **system-wide opportunity lattice**, not merely one future trading specialist.

Its role is to:
- generate high-density opportunity events;
- provide market-state context;
- recover opportunity after an initially wrong directional interpretation;
- reduce repeated wrong-direction trades;
- route clean opportunities to a future portfolio of roughly 6–16 specialists;
- reduce the amount of the R9 REAL -> SYNTH performance gap each specialist must recover individually.

## Lattice concept

The retained design hypothesis is a layered lattice in which different scales have different jobs.

Conceptual layers may include:
- higher-timeframe structural ownership;
- intermediate regime/state context;
- fast/scalping opportunity geometry;
- grid event clock;
- opportunity recovery/reclassification;
- bounded single-trade ownership;
- specialist routing.

The architecture is **multi-timeframe aware**, but timeframes must have distinct jobs rather than simple voting.

The architecture is intended to become **multi-session aware**, including relevant global liquidity regimes such as:
- Australia/Oceania;
- Japan;
- China/Asia;
- Russia / Eastern-Europe-linked liquidity where evidence supports distinct XAUUSD behavior;
- Middle East;
- continental Europe;
- United Kingdom/London;
- New York/US.

These are context hypotheses, not assumptions that every region must become a separate hard filter.

The architecture may later become **economic-event aware** for high/medium-impact information relevant to:
- gold;
- United States;
- Asia;
- Europe.

Event awareness should modify state/risk/interpretation when evidence supports it, not merely disable trading by default.

## Immediate research priority

The highest current red flags are:
1. GROSS LOSS;
2. DRAWDOWN.

The first research hunt is therefore deliberately narrow.

Do not simultaneously launch broad session, news, specialist, HTF, scalping, and portfolio research.

First seek mechanisms/structures that materially reduce gross loss and drawdown **without solving the problem merely by deleting most trades**.

## Opportunity-recovery requirement

Opportunity recovery is a first-class objective.

Research should seek mechanisms that can:
- recognize degradation/failure of the initial directional thesis;
- avoid repeated same-direction mistakes;
- reclassify a live opportunity as continuation, mean reversion, or abstention;
- recover opportunity causally without Martingale;
- preserve high event throughput where justified.

Recovery is not permission to average into losses.

## Creative-hypothesis rule

Creative synthesis is encouraged.

Invented profit claims, fabricated citations, or pretending a hypothetical mechanism is publicly validated are prohibited.

## Discovery success philosophy

Prefer a large structural improvement that changes the research frontier over many micro-successes.

Meaningful evidence may include:
- material gross-loss reduction;
- material drawdown reduction;
- improved adverse-path handling;
- improved wrong-direction recovery;
- preserved or improved trade/opportunity velocity;
- a mechanism that unlocks a stronger downstream specialist architecture.

A small improvement is worth keeping when it unlocks a larger mechanism, but it must not become the research destination.

## Benchmark

R9 SYNTH remains the guiding-light performance frontier, not an execution input.

The current Delta-A-alpha research priority is to make the system-wide lattice reduce the burden on future specialists while moving toward or beyond the R9 SYNTH total-system profile.

## Timeout-safe rule

One bounded research family at a time.

Before an expensive test or broad source hunt:
- persist the question;
- persist useful source mechanisms;
- persist reusable helpers;
- persist negative results;
- advance the cursor before beginning the next family.

Do not let chat become the only location of research state.
