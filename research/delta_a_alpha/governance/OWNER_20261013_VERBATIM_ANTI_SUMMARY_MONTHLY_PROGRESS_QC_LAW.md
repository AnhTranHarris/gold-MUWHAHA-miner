2. **Every accepted mechanism must have an original-source manifest:** exact file paths and Git SHA / content hashes; raw original tick-data checksum and coverage; starting capital and lot/margin/commission/slippage assumptions; full original settings; feature availability/as-of timestamps; executable replay command; original trade ledger and per-day/per-ISO-week/per-month balance/equity/gross-loss metrics; provenance and incremental improvement evidence. Do not reconstruct or silently alter previously accepted code from a textual account.
3. **Seven month folders are separate evaluation/research partitions, never a model feature or runtime calendar routing switch.** January discoveries carry forward causally and are checked in subsequent month(s); the eventual single engine must infer regimes from information known at decision time.
4. **Two reference roles remain distinct:** R9 SYNTH is the aspirational day/week/month whole-system performance goal (100% target, evaluated period-by-period, including cash, completed velocity, loss containment and DD); R9 OVERFIT is an offline per-trade teacher (75% target separately for entry/hold/exit/profit/drawdown/gross loss). NEVER feed future R9 outcomes into live/causal decisions. References are not the Dukascopy execution feed.
5. **Python original Dukascopy tick chronology remains validated and canonical.** Derive all requested bars directly from ordered original UTC ticks. Do not repeat broad Dukascopy audits or MT5 report audits as substitutes for strategy work. If a novel concrete discrepancy is discovered, isolate its exact scope and evidence; no speculative chart-clock offsets.
6. **Monthly evidence completeness:** immutable baseline and versions; original source and settings; exact experimental hypotheses (including negative/null tests in technical records); changed logic; entry/hold/exit breakdown; booked and floating equity; daily, ISO-week and monthly closed-deal cash; session/hour; net, gross profit/loss, win rate, PF, trade count, expectancy, balance/equity DD; source unique IDs; blocker/failure notes and QC check results.
