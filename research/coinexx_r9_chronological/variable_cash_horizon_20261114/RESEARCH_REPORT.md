## Advanced, hypothesis-led regime and nested-trend formulas

The operational `QuoteRegime` computes strictly causal completed-second signals with no true trade volume/depth:

For BUY/SELL sign `s`, last midquote `m`, lag h=3,10,30,120 seconds, and completed quote-second squared-return volatility `sigma_1s`, use

    Z_h = tanh(s * (m_t - m_(t-h)) / max(sqrt(h)*sigma_1s, quoted_spread/2, one_price_cent))

Define nested impulse persistence vs reversal:

    I = .25 Z_3 + .35 Z_10 + .30 Z_30 + .10 Z_120
        - .20 max(0, spread_current/median_spread_30 - 1)
        - .03 max(0, previous_quote_arrivals/current_second_arrivals - 2)
        - .15 * 1{sign disagreement across horizons}

This is a NONCALIBRATED confidence index, NOT ROC-AUC or P(net winner). Decisions abstain if completed second history is stale, time anchors are missing, intrasecond quote-gap >2.5 sec, or activity is inadequate. The experimental arm may preserve a runner only once the current stop can legally lock +$0.50 modeled net; this index has NO demonstrated statistical holding advantage. Quote arrivals and spread are liquidity *proxies* only, not traded volume or order-book depth.

**Next statistically valid formula:** fit conditional **policy-specific** cash competing risks in predeclared quote-availability/trend regimes on strictly earlier months, with hierarchical shrinkage instead of sparse overfit session buckets:

    F_{k,r,a}(u|z) = integral_{0..u} S_{r,a}(v-|z) dLambda_{k,r,a}(v|z)

Actions `a={CLOSE_NOW, HOLD_EXISTING_STOP, LEGAL_TIGHTEN}` and distinct causes `k={+0.50 net realized, adverse cash stop, quote-gap right censor, other exit}`. Include current executable cash and lifetime max favorable/adverse excursion; condition on `P5 entry` only as a predictor, not as P(profitable hold). Calibrate conditional mean and downside by earlier blocks, with effective-sample reliability discount:

    Q_a(t) = sum_k F_{k,r,a} E[realized_net_cash_k|z]
             - lambda * CVaR95(loss_cash|r,a,z)
             - eta * E[foregone_new_rearm_cash | state, action]
             - xi * sqrt(var(Q_a)/n_eff(r,a))

Require a conservative, previously evaluated lower bound of `Q_hold-Q_exit` >0 AND preserve predeclared >=95% per-month trade retention, positive after-cost cash per active hour, no worse gross loss budget, worst-day cash, account peak DD or CVaR. If no credible conditional benefit, take available +0.50 and leave stop unchanged until a protective exit. No subseries can be treated as independently observed order-book liquidity. Freeze the parameters before evaluating May–Sep; Jan–Sep repeatedly research-exposed, not pristine OOS.

| Native indicator | Observable from original Coinexx Bid/Ask timestamps? | Proper narrow role / warning |
|---|---|---|

| `iStdDev` M1/M5 shift=1 | **Yes**, completed Bid OHLC close | Quiet-to-expansion relative volatility, compared to raw quote realized variance |
| `iBands` M1/M5 shift=1 | **Yes**, completed Bid close | Price compression/expansion proxy, but potential collinearity with iStdDev |
| `iAMA`/`iFrAMA` M1/M5 shift=1 | **Yes**, completed Bid close | Adaptive trend/efficiency / fractal trend candidate; require demonstrated incremental profit, not another feature sweep |
| `iSAR` shift=1 | **Yes**, price OHLC | Optional comparative stop price only; native indicator level can be stale vs second-scale quote exit |
| `iVolumes(VOLUME_TICK)` and `iVolume` | **Only quote-count proxy**; source `volume` is zero, not real lots | Quote-arrival activity; original MT5 chart tick_volume may differ from raw ticklogger due quote reception and compression; validate parity |
| `iVolumes(VOLUME_REAL)`, `iOBV`/`iMFI` as true volume, VWAP with executed units, VPIN, genuine OFI, order-book imbalance/microprice | **No reliable input**; original Coinexx `last`, `volume`,`volume_real` zero/empty | Never present price revisions or quote ticks as executed flows/depth. MQL5 indicators return HANDLES, not directly values; require `CopyBuffer(handle,buffer,1,count,...)` / sufficient `BarsCalculated` and explicit current-bar exclusion. Native MT5 indicator values may differ from Python as-of Bid-bar reconstruction when broker bar histories or tick processing differ; numerical parity mandatory before any EA integration.
