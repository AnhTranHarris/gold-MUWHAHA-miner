## Why the next stage is NOT another bad-risk cutoff grid
The previous F401-R118H frozen same-month teacher-risk correlation was strongest within neutral errors; opposite-error association was near zero outside January. R118I-P1 demonstrated that a 335D causal snapshot with 2/8/30s privileged future-path auxiliary loss has no protected selectivity on January VAL. R118I-P1 excluded 31.9% of training events because its future-path target required a near-instantaneous quote after every horizon; this quote-drought censoring can create selection bias. The next architecture changes *observation likelihood*, *state transition* and *supervision*, rather than changing thresholds/leaves. It can represent the absence of quotes explicitly as a censored outcome.

## Primary novel mechanism: continuous-time quote-auction state and competing hazards
1. **Causal stream input:** true source-published best BID/ASK revisions, elapsed time, spread, one-sided/joint update marks, Dukascopy quote-provided size (NEVER claimed as traded volume), completed M1/M5/M15/H1/H8 bar-published geometry, original BUY/SELL and UTC session. Every quote at or after the evaluated original t0 must be invisible.
2. **Streaming self-supervision on Jan FIT:** a 96- or 128-state continuous-time decay recurrent cell updates on EVERY prior quote mark; predict next mark (BID-only/ASK-only/joint/no change), waiting-time distribution with survival censoring, and future mid-price path across 2/8/30/60 seconds with missing quote endpoints masked not dropped. Forecast exogenous price path independently from the sign of the original signal.
3. **Regime router:** learned 6-state mixture (liquidity squeeze, trend continuation, oscillatory sweep, absorption/reclaim, quote drought, shock transition) using 1m–8h completed geometry plus quote update-state; names are hypotheses, not verified labels. Fit mixture on FIT only. Avoid routing by look-ahead states and avoid fixed daily historical percentiles.
4. **Three *distinct* conditional experts:** (a) opposite-direction directional failure conditioned on predicted path first passage and support/resistance phase; (b) neutral/non-resolution risk conditioned on quote mark intensity and censored wait; (c) correct-opportunity guardian trained on independent survival/continuation target, with independent capacity and output (NOT simply 1-bad).
5. **Protected veto arbitration:** if both directional-risk and neutral-risk experts classify the original signal as high-risk AND the correct guardian does not identify a valuable live opportunity, permit original-t0 veto. Optimize *wrong veto count* first subject to a hard correct-loss ceiling, then conditional side/session/hour concentration, original supply quota and original F234 marginal overlap. A later phase may consider quote-time-specific cancellation ONLY for still-unsubmitted orders, not retroactive t0 fills.
6. **Density-first benchmark:** Compare dense next-mark/time/path likelihood plus original signal wrong-veto capacity against frozen R118H and R118I-P1. Out-of-sample month scores must include all 1,266,934 original event IDs, with source-exact 0/1/2 labels and unfittable rows default to KEEP. Never use original same-month teacher scores or futures at inference.
