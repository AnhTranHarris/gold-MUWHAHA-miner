This is a workflow-method defect, not retroactive invalidation of previously owner-approved research-only components.

## 4. Hard asymmetry: wrong removals dominate, good losses are not a co-equal objective

Track the 2x2 counts **for each of eight broker-months** on the ORIGINAL immutable denominator: originally correct kept and vetoed; originally incorrect/neutral kept and vetoed, split incorrect into opposite vs neutral. Optimize for incorrect rejection with correct retention as a strict risk constraint, not (wrong veto - correct veto) equally weighted or a symmetric objective. Publish false-veto cost, true-veto yield and marginal **wrong-to-good veto ratio**, incremental wrong-veto *precision* = bad/(bad+good), correctly directed retained, correct rescue and bad re-entry, same-hour random placebo, causal ablation and supply quotas. High-capacity models should explicitly classify asymmetric wrong risk, not just maximize generic accuracy.

Frozen F16 current benchmark (F234): original signals 2,574,251; correct 1,208,071, wrong/neutral 1,366,180; correct retained 998,996 (82.69348%), wrong rejected 295,618 (21.63829%). Pooled original target: correct retention >=71% and wrong veto >=90%; additional wrong required at least **933,944**, remaining *pooled* permissible extra good losses at most **141,265**, implying **>=6.61 additional wrong removals per extra good loss** if there is no rescue. DK July F16 retained 88,343/130,408 correct (~67.74%) and needs at least **4,247 correct-source recoveries** just to reach 71% before extra losses. A strict veto-only sequence cannot satisfy this cell.

Research target: prioritize >=6.61:1 *incremental pooled* wrong/good selectivity where rescue is absent, with >=8:1 and >=10:1 as stretch discovery objectives, but do not label these ratios formally owner-approved per-function gates or manufacture a false guaranteed feasible result. Do not automatically reject a scientifically informative component that fails a stretch target; keep it **RESEARCH ONLY / unpromoted** and use it in a stronger cooperative mechanism.
