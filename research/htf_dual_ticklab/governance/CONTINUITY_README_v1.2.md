# v1.2 Continuity Supplement

Authoritative code: `htf_ticklab/continuity_guard.py`. Tests: `tests/test_continuity_guard.py`.

This is an **additive** policy to v1.0 and v1.1; no old law, investor rule, source restriction, or data registration is deleted. The new continuity checks do not replace any market-execution tests or the integrated EA systems judge.

- Use byte-backed evidence and a verified lineage journal; summaries are only navigational.
- Never reset research just because a new chat starts or a message times out.
- For scored research, retrieve the complete experiment and original artifacts; if missing, block and recover.
- Check source files from authorized registries and corrected UTC, and verify the approved GitHub HEAD and ancestry externally.
- Read `CROSS_CUTTING_CONTINUITY_LAW_v1.2.md` and `NEW_CHAT_BOOTSTRAP_PROTOCOL_v1.2.md` before proceeding.
- Original chat transcripts may be unavailable from another session; this protocol depends on durable full artifacts, frozen plans, decision journals, and records saved separately while the current chat is accessible.

**No summary-only research decisions and no invented research continuity.**
