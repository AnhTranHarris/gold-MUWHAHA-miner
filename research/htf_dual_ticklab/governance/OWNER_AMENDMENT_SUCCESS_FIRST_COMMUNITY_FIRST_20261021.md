## 1. Investor-identified failure: negative-research fixation

Stop optimizing research behavior for ever-more-exhaustive proof that a failed configuration fails. A failed *configuration* is evidence against that tested configuration under its exact inputs/split; it is not proof its entire predictive-mechanism family or all plausible cooperative adaptations are worthless. Avoid repetitive failure audits, unproductive brute-force grids, rebuilding proven engines, and substituting more iterations of the same architecture for a genuinely new mechanism. Do not label weak negative outcomes as successful research progress.

Research must deliberately exploit the **strongest verified positive source-native discoveries**, alongside creative exploration. Good results must not be discarded merely because an adjacent configuration failed. Findings are measured and qualified; no success is fabricated or cherry-picked.

For each original function/model, first inventory the already verified *positive* source-event controls and accepted helpers, with exact source hashes/masks and limits. Rank by incremental bad-veto precision, retained-correct benefit, error-class uniqueness and orthogonality, covered broker-months, supply safety, causal availability, and implementation cost; not by claimed standalone profit.

Maintain a **WORKING_MECHANISM_REGISTER** with: pinned positive mechanism; what it gets right; what incorrect subclass/side/market state it misses; causal input dependencies; accepted-owner status; what complementary mechanism can enhance it. Prioritize:
1. **Exploit:** strengthen the strongest existing valid signal-veto behavior by condition-aware features, calibrated thresholds, broker/side gates, and MT5-feasible state interactions.
2. **Combine:** test complementary positive components with nonredundant errors, classwise consensus, veto agreement, or correctly directed recovery; benchmark against frozen F234 cascade.

Never delete or rewrite positive historical source evidence. No unapproved atom becomes a checklist acceptance without original proof and owner signoff.

## 4. Failure-handling brake: fast diagnosis, then novel successor

For a clearly failed tested configuration:
- Preserve full run, source/hash/split and all unfavorable outcomes in Drive; publish a concise blocker.
- Diagnose **once at the mechanism level**: leakage/clock violation, wrong subclass not captured, overrejection of correct signals, cross-broker drift, quota failure, feature unavailable at decision time, or MT5 timing infeasibility.
- Decide if a specific, source-backed modification could substantially increase selectivity. If yes, require materially different information/mechanism/interaction before the next expensive test.
- If no credible improvement mechanism, **park the variant** and return to the highest verified positive lead or a distinct community-sourced hypothesis. Do not audit that negative repeatedly or grid-search tiny threshold perturbations pretending they are independent breakthroughs.
- Reopen parked paths only on genuinely new data, identified causal features, orthogonal mechanisms or a material source-code defect. Failure evidence remains retained, but cannot monopolize cycles.

Ablations and falsification are still mandatory; the amendment prohibits *wasteful repetition*, not honest reporting or critical testing.
