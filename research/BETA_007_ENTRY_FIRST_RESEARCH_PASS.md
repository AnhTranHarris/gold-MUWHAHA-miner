Economic decomposition for test design:

- **Winning trade count** = number of admitted entries × observed positive-exit fraction, under *the same* fees, exposure and exit contract. Evaluate both factors so a 90% win rate from suppressing 99% of entries does not count as progress.
- **ENTRY** sets conditional distribution of immediate quote-cost-adjusted markouts, MAE and available future MFE (latter two diagnostic, never signal features). **HOLD** decides whether the original event can survive to subsequent favorable movement. **EXIT** decides gain captured or loss realized. Their effects interact and cannot be summed as independent profits.
- A completed lower/higher bar can inform entry only once observable on a subsequent original tick. Use the 17-layer hierarchy: TICK, 250ms, 1s, 5s, 15s, 30s, 45s, M1, M5, M7, M15, M30, M45, H1, H4, H12, D1. Every entry and fill still executes on the exact ordered Bid/Ask tick.
- Dukascopy rows provide *quotes and quoted volumes*, not exchange depth or genuine trade-by-trade aggressor signed volume. A signed midquote-change / quote-burst signal is a **quote-pressure proxy**; do not call it true order-flow imbalance, market depth or verified resting liquidity. Actual structural 'liquidity sweep' means **price traded/quoted through a constructed swing reference then reclaimed**, not proof of unseen large orders.

## Candidate entry-focused mechanism families (PRERESEARCH, not certified)

1. **BRK-RETEST-ACCEPT:** derive H/L from a completed prior range (session, rolling five M1, or higher structural owner); tick crosses H+buffer or L−buffer; tick returns to frozen level zone; only *renewed acceptance on the observed quote* triggers continuation. A high-timeframe ownership gate (e.g. completed M15/H1) may be studied separately. Compare earliest break vs delayed retest on the **matched underlying breakout cohort**, opportunity retention and state age. Hold/Exit frozen for isolated entry comparison.
2. **SWEEP-RECLAIM-FAILED-IGNITION:** use the same frozen break boundary; quote crosses outside then moves back through by a measured cushion within a fixed clock window; countertrade only when failure becomes observable. Test absence of future swing-pivot information, countertrend risk, and if the rescue tail under persistent strong trends overwhelms small wins. Compare allowed sweep depth, first-failure latency, and protective exits.
3. **PULLBACK-REACCELERATE:** completed M15/H1 directional ownership (trend EMA/swing state) defines eligible side, completed M5/M1 defines valid pullback into a causal volatility-normalized band, then 1s/250ms tick sequence provides renewed impulse. No lagging candle OHLC is treated as an execution tape. Distinguish trend continuation from roundtrip spread and late chasing; compare raw momentum entries.
4. **RANGE-EDGE-FADE:** observed causal compression/range state plus distance from recent completed swing/range boundary; buy reclaim at lower zone, sell upper rejection, with hard regime invalidation on persistent one-way displacement. Potential high hit rate may disappear under wider spreads or sustained trends. Cannot call it the same signal as failed-ignition without ablation.
5. **GATES SHARED BUT NOT DIRECTIONAL:** normalize min spread against expected price excursion/horizon and trailing realized quote-volatility; block abnormal quote gaps and unreliable high-variance/event spread spikes; add ATR/ADX only for clearly distinct purposes (volatility vs trending strength), measured via controlled ablation. Spread alone cannot make bad directional entries good; suppressing most trades is not a successful high-velocity system.
6. **SHARED ACCOUNT ROUTER:** candidate specialists compete for a single live account; one position/exposure policy, deterministic arbitration on conflicting simultaneous signals and correlated losses, cash/equity/stop-out continuity across month boundaries. Individual signal accuracy or overlapping event cohort P&L must NEVER be summed to claim total system profit.

Suggested sequential tests: (A) causal entry cohort signals with H=5/15/30/60sec markout labels and MFE/MAE diagnostics, same execution model and fixed exits; (B) matched entry cohorts through several causal hold/exit alternatives; (C) full combined chronological broker-aware account; (D) Jan–Jul month-by-month, spread/slippage stress and valid forward walls. Predeclare trade retention floor before tuning. Success requires a reproducible actual same-feed benefit and owner gate, not merely rhetoric about synergy.
