## B. Community sources and reproducible additions

1. **Confirmed structure vs false break.** [MQL5 Chinese BoS guide](https://www.mql5.com/zh/articles/15017) requires a **completed candle BODY close** outside a confirmed prior swing to call it a valid BoS, not a transient wick. [MQL5 original closed-fractal tutorial](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/19365) confirms right-hand pivots before use. **Mechanism:** store pivot high/low plus *confirmation timestamp*, distinguish WICK_SWEEP_PENDING, BREAK_CLOSE_CONFIRMED, RETURN_INSIDE and successful/failed RETEST. Never backdate the pivot, wick or completion.
2. **Stateful structural zones, not one magic line.** [Chinese TradingView market-structure index](https://cn.tradingview.com/scripts/market-structure/) describes clustered historical pivot regions, ATR-scaled buffers, frozen pre-break levels and a state machine waiting for the subsequent retest before labeling role reversal. A faithful independent implementation can use deterministic within-ATR pivot-level clustering and explicit state transition timestamps; data-mined reaction quality must use only PAST reactions. Avoid importing advertised source ranking/performance as evidence. This is an ADDITIONAL reconstructible experiment for the BETA016 S2 and S4 families.
3. **Multi-regime router.** [MQL5 regime indicator](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/17737) and [adaptive EA followup](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/17781) separate trend, ranging and volatility before choosing a specialist. This is source-code architecture, not verified XAUUSD outcome. Strip all variable lot sizing. [Reddit, Feb 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/algotrading/comments/1r470sy/) discusses ATR-compression vs expansion specialists; [Reddit, March 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/algotrading/comments/1rvfy12/) warns regime filters can overfit or trade too little. No absolute cross-strategy score or benefit assumed.
4. **Volatility != direction.** [Korean-language MQL5 explanation](https://www.mql5.com/ko/articles/10748) explicitly notes ATR measures range intensity, not trend direction; combine it with a separate directional/structure dimension instead of treating an ATR rise as bullish. [Japanese open-source multi-frame ATR dashboard](https://jp.tradingview.com/script/anybMGQQ-Multi-Timeframe-ATR-Monitor/) provides independent M1/M5/M15/H1/D1 volatility horizons. One article translated across languages is not independent experimental replication.
5. **Session breakout is only a family, not a proof.** [ForexFactory gold NY ORB post](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/post/15609751) specifies a session-frozen first five-minute range, candle-body/ATR confirmation and first valid close. Its high win-rate/scale-out claims are self-reported and cannot be imported into BETA. The poster's partial-position closures conflict with the **fixed single 0.01-lot position**; reconstruct only setup signals and compare fixed original Hold/Exit. [ForexFactory alternative results](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/post/14705801) illustrate practitioners sometimes fail to find stable breakouts.
6. **Bid/Ask quote-cost and quote intensity.** [Market Microstructure Analytics open script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/34R4Mh5W-Market-Microstructure-Analytics/) details spread dynamics, but some methods need TRUE transaction volume/order-book depth. Our original Dukascopy feed has ordered Bid/Ask quote updates; compute **actual quoted spread** and **quote-update/interarrival intensity**, NOT market traded volume, signed order flow, depth, microprice or stop clusters. A possible S7 veto: rising quote event rate + widening spread despite low directional midprice efficiency; avoid paying adverse selection on a nominal breakout.
7. **Multiscale structural break buffering.** [Chinese-language Regime Blocks script](https://cn.tradingview.com/script/ufMHeB9Z-Regime-Blocks/) uses ATR-scaled distance beyond last confirmed swing and persistent directional regime. [Japanese Break Confirm indicator](https://jp.tradingview.com/script/mLpKebfD-Break-Confirm-ATR-Body/) combines prior confirmed pivot, breakout candle body and wicks, ATR and EMAs. Separately preregister close-confirmed vs faster tick-confirmed branches, not a backtest with retrospective closing price at earlier ticks.
