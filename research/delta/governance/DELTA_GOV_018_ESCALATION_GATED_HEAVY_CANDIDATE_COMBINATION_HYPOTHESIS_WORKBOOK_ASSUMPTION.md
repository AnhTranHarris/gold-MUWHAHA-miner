# DELTA GOV-018 — Escalation-Gated Heavy Candidate Combination Hypothesis Workbook Assumption

**Effective:** 2026-10-02  
**Branch:** `delta`  
**Status:** MANDATORY / HUMAN-REVISABLE ASSUMPTION  
**Layer:** RESEARCH ESCALATION / CANDIDATE FUSION  
**Default state:** DORMANT  
**Research metrics:** NOT DEFINED BY THIS CONTRACT  
**Heavy combination workbook:** NOT ACTIVE

## 1. Purpose

DELTA assumes that a viable system candidate may sometimes emerge not from a single discovered mechanism, but from a **causal combination, merger, or synthesis of multiple candidate mechanisms**.

Because this search can consume substantially more spreadsheet, reasoning, and replay resources than ordinary candidate harvesting, DELTA must keep it on a separate, explicitly activated research surface.

The ordinary GOV-014 quant-harvesting workbook remains the default first-line research surface.

## 2. Two-stage spreadsheet architecture

### Stage 1 — Standard candidate harvesting

Use the normal GOV-014 spreadsheet-first loop:

`HARVEST -> SLICE -> INTERACTION -> SPECULATE -> RANK -> PREREGISTER -> TICK REPLAY -> WRITE BACK -> REPEAT`

This stage searches for viable individual candidates or bounded structural packages without launching unrestricted combinatorial search.

### Stage 2 — Heavy candidate-combination hallucination workbook

A **separate Google Spreadsheet** must be used when the heavy combination stage is activated.

It must not be embedded as extra tabs in the ordinary candidate-harvesting workbook.

The heavy workbook exists to deliberately generate and rank custom synthesized formulas, rules, routers, or specialist compositions derived from multiple candidate components.

## 3. Activation rules

### Mandatory fallback activation

If the initial GOV-014 spreadsheet process is completed through reasonable bounded cycles and **fails to yield a viable candidate for system integration**, DELTA must explicitly activate the heavy candidate-combination workbook as the next research surface.

The failure declaration must be durable and must identify why the initial workbook did not produce an integration-viable candidate.

### Early owner activation

The owner may explicitly direct DELTA to activate the heavy candidate-combination workbook before Stage 1 is formally exhausted.

An explicit owner directive is sufficient because it is a human-controlled resource escalation.

### Community or research indication

Community sources or research findings may indicate that combining or merging candidate mechanisms could be favorable.

Such evidence may create a **COMBINATION_ESCALATION_FLAG**, but does not by itself launch the heavy workbook before Stage 1 failure.

Before Stage 1 failure, actual activation from a community/research flag requires owner authorization.

## 4. Meaning of "hallucination" in this contract

"Hallucination" means **deliberate speculative synthesis**, not fabricated evidence.

The heavy workbook may aggressively generate candidate combinations that have not yet been observed directly in community sources.

Every generated combination must be labeled:

`HYPOTHESIS_ONLY`

until it survives causal reconstruction and tick-level replay.

No invented backtest result, source claim, metric, or market fact may be represented as evidence.

## 5. Allowed combination inputs

Combination inputs may originate from:

- owner-directed candidate ideas;
- reconstructible community mechanisms;
- independently discovered DELTA candidates;
- bounded spreadsheet findings;
- causal tick-replay findings;
- specialist components;
- session-aware components;
- news/event-aware components;
- trend-within-trend components;
- entry components;
- initial-hold components;
- later authorized holding/exit components;
- other reconstructible rule components.

Every input component must retain lineage to its source, candidate ID, or research artifact.

## 6. Candidate fusion operations

The heavy workbook may deliberately explore combinations such as:

- pairwise candidate fusion;
- three-way or larger bounded candidate fusion;
- conditional AND / OR / NOT rule composition;
- weighted score formulas;
- piecewise formulas;
- regime-conditioned formulas;
- session-conditioned formulas;
- news-conditioned formulas;
- trend-within-trend-conditioned formulas;
- temporal sequencing of components;
- specialist ownership transfer;
- router arbitration;
- fallback logic;
- state-machine composition;
- formula mutation;
- threshold interaction;
- conflict resolution;
- candidate component substitution;
- partial component borrowing;
- complementary-strength recombination.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

## 7. Custom formula candidate

The purpose of the heavy workbook is not merely to rank existing candidates.

It may create a **new custom formula candidate** whose logic is a synthesized combination of multiple components.

A custom formula candidate must preserve:

- explicit component lineage;
- exact formula or state logic;
- causal timing;
- input variables;
- timeframe dependencies;
- specialist ownership where applicable;
- activation conditions;
- invalidation conditions;
- expected interaction rationale;
- provenance of every non-speculative ingredient.

A custom formula candidate becomes a test candidate only after preregistration.

## 8. Search discipline

The heavy workbook must search broadly enough to expose non-obvious interactions, but remain bounded.

Preferred techniques may include:

- candidate-by-candidate interaction matrices;
- pairwise and triadic contribution maps;
- complementary-strength / complementary-failure matrices;
- conditional interaction tables;
- formula mutation tables;
- rule-sequencing matrices;
- regime ownership maps;
- component-ablation comparisons;
- counterfactual merge tests;
- conflict-resolution tables;
- candidate lineage graphs;
- HYPOTHESIS_ONLY mutation queues.

The workbook should emphasize **structured combinatorial search**, not uncontrolled random enumeration.

## 9. Resource controls

Because this mode is intentionally resource-heavy:

- it must use a separate spreadsheet file;
- activation must be explicit and durable;
- raw tick dumps remain prohibited;
- event/trade/slice summaries remain preferred;
- candidate families should be sharded when the workbook grows large;
- combinatorial expansion should be staged;
- low-information branches should be pruned;
- duplicate formulas must be deduplicated;
- equivalent logical formulas should be normalized where practical;
- only the strongest synthesized candidates should enter expensive tick replay.

The heavy workbook must not become a substitute for the tick engine.

## 10. Relationship to GOV-014 thresholds

GOV-014 remains the primary candidate-screening contract.

The heavy workbook does not weaken:

- existing metric gates;
- existing SYNTH-gap logic;
- lock rules;
- hard deterioration rules;
- Stage-A requirements;
- causal replay requirements.

It exists to generate stronger candidate structures when ordinary harvesting does not produce a viable integration candidate.

## 11. Combination evidence hierarchy

A synthesized combination should distinguish:

1. **SOURCE-SUPPORTED COMPONENT** — reconstructible from public/community material;
2. **OWNER-DIRECTED COMPONENT** — explicitly supplied by the owner;
3. **RESEARCH-SUPPORTED COMPONENT** — supported by prior causal DELTA findings;
4. **HYPOTHESIS_ONLY INTERACTION** — speculative combination relationship;
5. **TICK-REPLAY CONFIRMED INTERACTION** — combination behavior confirmed causally.

The workbook must not collapse these categories into one evidence label.

## 12. No automatic integration

A promising spreadsheet combination is not automatically integrated into DELTA.

The required path remains:

`HEAVY WORKBOOK HYPOTHESIS -> RANK -> PREREGISTER -> DUKASCOPY TICK REPLAY -> WRITE BACK -> PROMOTION REVIEW`

Only causally replay-confirmed candidates may advance.

## 13. Failure of the heavy workbook

If the heavy workbook also fails to produce a viable candidate, DELTA must not endlessly expand combinations.

The next step should be an explicit structural review under GOV-007, potentially including:

- STRUCTURAL_CHANGE;
- SPECIALIST_SPLIT;
- NEW_SPECIALIST;
- RECOMBINATION redesign;
- ROUTER_CHANGE;
- new reconstructible public mechanism search.

Further combinatorial expansion requires a new justification.
