# DELTA GOV-018 — Resource-Gated Heavy Quant Candidate Synthesis and Speculative Combination Assumption

**Effective:** 2026-10-02  
**Branch:** `delta`  
**Status:** MANDATORY / HUMAN-REVISABLE ASSUMPTION  
**Layer:** CANDIDATE SYNTHESIS / ESCALATION ARCHITECTURE  
**Research metrics:** NOT DEFINED BY THIS CONTRACT  
**Candidate research:** NOT STARTED BY THIS CONTRACT  
**Heavy synthesis workbook:** SEPARATE / DORMANT UNTIL ACTIVATED

## 1. Purpose

DELTA may use a separate, resource-intensive speculative synthesis workflow when evidence suggests that combining, merging, gating, routing, or mathematically transforming multiple candidate mechanisms may produce a materially better system candidate than any parent candidate alone.

The heavy synthesis workflow exists to generate **high-divergence, senior-quant-style composite hypotheses** while preserving causality, reconstructibility, attribution, and bounded compute.

This contract does not itself create a trading candidate, authorize MQL5 coding, unseal August 2026, or begin candidate testing.

## 2. Separate workbook required

Heavy candidate synthesis must use a spreadsheet that is separate from the normal DELTA Candidate Quant Harvesting workbook.

The normal workbook remains the default low-to-moderate resource research surface.

The heavy workbook is an escalation instrument for combination research and must not silently become the default path.

Canonical heavy workbook title:

`DELTA Heavy Quant Candidate Synthesis Template`

## 3. Activation rules

The heavy synthesis workbook is DORMANT by default.

It becomes eligible for explicit activation under any of these conditions:

### A. OWNER_TRIGGER

The owner explicitly directs DELTA to combine, merge, synthesize, graft, route, or mathematically integrate candidate mechanisms.

### B. EVIDENCE_TRIGGER

Community sources or DELTA research expose a **specific reconstructible interaction hypothesis** indicating that two or more candidate mechanisms may be complementary.

Examples include:

- one candidate captures a regime that another misses;
- two candidates fail in different, weakly overlapping slices;
- one candidate improves entry quality while another improves initial-hold survival;
- candidate signals appear conditionally useful under different session/news/trend states;
- a transparent interaction term or state-dependent weighting has a plausible causal mechanism;
- a router or mixture-of-specialists may resolve candidate conflicts better than either parent alone.

A vague belief that "combining things might help" is not enough. The proposed relationship must be describable and testable.

### C. INITIAL_WORKBOOK_FAILURE_TRIGGER — MANDATORY

If the normal spreadsheet-first DELTA quant-harvesting cycle fails to yield a viable candidate for system integration after its bounded research cycle, the heavy synthesis workbook becomes a **mandatory explicit escalation step** before abandoning the candidate family or declaring the search exhausted.

This is the principal fallback trigger.

## 4. Meaning of "hallucination" in this contract

The owner's term "hallucination" is implemented as **deliberate speculative hypothesis generation**.

It means DELTA is encouraged to generate unusual, non-obvious, cross-candidate mathematical structures, including ideas that are not directly stated in community sources.

It never means DELTA may fabricate:

- empirical results;
- backtests;
- community claims;
- source citations;
- formulas supposedly published by others;
- causal evidence;
- performance statistics.

Every speculative construction is labeled:

`HYPOTHESIS_ONLY`

until causal replay provides evidence.

## 5. Senior-quant synthesis modes

The heavy workbook may generate and rank reconstructible candidate combinations using techniques such as:

- additive score combinations;
- weighted mixtures;
- multiplicative interaction terms;
- conditional interaction terms;
- saturating or bounded nonlinear transforms;
- piecewise state-dependent formulas;
- regime-dependent weights;
- specialist routers;
- candidate veto / override relationships;
- confidence- or uncertainty-weighted mixtures;
- residual correction terms;
- orthogonal candidate stacking;
- failure-slice complementarity;
- conditional ensemble voting;
- hierarchical gating;
- state-machine grafting;
- candidate DNA recombination;
- symbolic formula mutation;
- counterfactual term removal/addition;
- adversarial sign inversion;
- monotonic transforms;
- volatility/session/news/trend-conditioned blending;
- sparse composite formulas;
- Pareto-front candidate recombination.

These are hypothesis-generation families only. No technique is presumed profitable.

## 6. Candidate provenance required

Every synthesized candidate must retain explicit lineage.

At minimum record:

- synthesized candidate ID;
- parent candidate IDs and exact versions;
- parent source/config hashes when available;
- inherited components;
- mutated components;
- newly invented components;
- interaction terms;
- gating/router logic;
- selected timeframes;
- state dependencies;
- causal update order;
- expected mechanism;
- failure mode being targeted;
- HYPOTHESIS_ONLY status before replay.

No anonymous or opaque composite formula may advance.

## 7. Causal attribution must survive combination

Combining multiple mechanisms must not erase scientific attribution.

For every composite that advances, later evaluation must be able to compare:

- each parent alone;
- the combined candidate;
- the combined candidate minus each major component;
- important interaction terms on/off;
- important routing/gating decisions on/off where feasible.

The objective is to distinguish genuine synergy from:

- one parent dominating the result;
- redundant components;
- accidental curve fitting;
- trade starvation;
- leverage expansion;
- hidden timing advantage;
- interaction terms that add no value.

## 8. Resource-gated design

Because this workflow is intentionally expensive, it must remain bounded.

Default design envelope unless later evidence justifies expansion:

- active parent candidates per synthesis package: 2–5;
- speculative formulas/composites generated per synthesis cycle: approximately 25–100;
- formulas promoted from spreadsheet ranking to causal tick replay: normally no more than 5–12 per cycle;
- interaction matrices should remain bounded and sparse rather than exhaustive combinatorial explosions;
- raw ticks remain prohibited from Google Sheets;
- use event/trade/slice summaries, candidate outputs, bounded feature interactions, and formula scorecards;
- shard synthesis work by combination family when one workbook would become unwieldy;
- expensive tick replay occurs only after spreadsheet ranking/preregistration.

These are resource controls, not performance thresholds.

## 9. Relationship to GOV-014

GOV-014 remains the default spreadsheet-first workflow:

`HARVEST -> SLICE -> INTERACTION -> SPECULATE -> RANK -> PREREGISTER -> TICK REPLAY -> WRITE BACK -> REPEAT`

GOV-018 adds an escalation branch:

`NORMAL HARVESTING -> NO VIABLE INTEGRATION CANDIDATE OR EXPLICIT COMBINATION TRIGGER -> HEAVY SYNTHESIS WORKBOOK -> COMPOSITE HYPOTHESES -> ABLATION/RANKING -> PREREGISTER -> TICK REPLAY`

The heavy workbook does not replace GOV-014.

## 11. No automatic promotion

A spreadsheet-ranked composite is not a candidate success.

Before system integration it must pass the same causal research discipline as other DELTA candidates, including:

- preregistration;
- exact Stage-A window;
- Dukascopy tick-level replay;
- execution-surface assumptions;
- primary metric visibility;
- activity/trade-count visibility;
- causal timing;
- required sensitivity testing when appropriate;
- later promotion/lock governance.

The heavy workbook may produce a custom quant formula candidate, but the formula becomes evidence-backed only after replay.

## 12. Speculative formulas must be MT5-reconstructible

Any formula or router intended for possible later integration must remain implementable from explicit causal logic.

Allowed complexity is high.

Opaque irreproducibility is not.

A successful synthesis must be expressible with:

- exact inputs;
- exact transformations;
- exact timing visibility;
- exact parameters;
- exact state transitions;
- exact routing/weighting rules;
- deterministic handling of missing or conflicting state.

## 13. No hindsight synthesis

Heavy synthesis may use historical outcomes for **offline hypothesis discovery and ranking**, but no final executable formula may consume information that would not have existed at its decision time.

Future-derived labels may not leak into live inference variables.

Any offline outcome-informed selection must be followed by causal replay.

## 14. Community-source use

Community mechanisms remain subject to the DELTA reconstructibility requirement.

When community material motivates a merge:

- preserve the source link and source date;
- distinguish sourced logic from DELTA-invented logic;
- do not attribute DELTA-created interaction terms to the source;
- use only mechanisms that can be faithfully reconstructed enough for testing.

## 16. Final synthesis lock

A successful synthesized candidate cannot be considered locked merely because its formula ranks well in the spreadsheet.

The final lock must preserve:

- candidate ID/version;
- all parent IDs/versions;
- exact composite formula;
- exact router/gating logic;
- exact interaction terms;
- selected timeframes;
- causal timing;
- ablation evidence;
- replay configuration;
- replay result hashes;
- execution surface;
- Stage-A result;
- frozen Jan–Jul replay when required;
- sensitivity results;
- source commit SHA;
- MT5 reconstruction mapping when later authorized.

## 17. Cleanup

The heavy workbook and temporary synthesis artifacts follow the existing post-lock cleanup policy.

Disposable intermediate mutation tables, scratch exports, superseded matrices, and rejected formula blobs may be removed only after a final candidate is locked and reproducibility-critical lineage is preserved.

Preserve a compact rejected-combination summary so dead combinations are not repeatedly rediscovered.
