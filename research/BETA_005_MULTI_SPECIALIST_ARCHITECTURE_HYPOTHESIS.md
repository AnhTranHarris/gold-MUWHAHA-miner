## Research premise and falsifiability

The owner proposes that highly variable and noisy XAUUSD microstructure makes a high-trade-velocity EA more robust when it combines *different, complementary trading mechanisms* rather than depending on one universal strategy. These mechanisms may contain simple or complex ideas and be assigned to different structural/regime conditions. **This is a design hypothesis, not a demonstrated theorem:** a well-designed single strategy or a selective no-trade state could outperform a crowded portfolio. BETA must test single-specialist controls, incremental combinations, and the full shared account; retain only components whose economic value survives robust causal validation.

**Research objective:** maximize *after-cost, broker-feasible net economic return* subject to preregistered constraints on gross loss, drawdown, tail risk, exposure, trade opportunity retention, capital survival, and cross-month robustness. Trade frequency is a measured opportunity/throughput objective, **never a mandatory trade quota**. More trades are not inherently better.

## Candidate modular architecture (not approved strategies)

1. **Tick-first data/decision clock.** Original ordered Dukascopy Bid/Ask ticks, and 16 derived candle grids (`250ms`, `1s`, `5s`, `15s`, `30s`, `45s`, `M1`, `M5`, `M7`, `M15`, `M30`, `M45`, `H1`, `H4`, `H12`, `D1`) give **17 analysis layers including `TICK`**. Every order, cancellation, stop, target, trailing and state transition acts on ticks, never on assumed OHLC intrabar ordering. Derived features carry explicit last-known/close timestamps; no future bars or hindsight labels enter decisions.
2. **Causal market-state/structural ownership router.** Explicitly observable structural regime, volatility, direction, compression/expansion, and failure conditions decide which independently defined *candidate specialist* is eligible. Different horizons perform distinct jobs. The router also permits abstention. Rules and precedence must be deterministic, independently reproducible in MQL5, and measured separately from specialist entry quality.
3. **Independently testable candidate specialists.** Explore, but do not presume profitability from: (a) trend continuation / pullback reacceleration; (b) structural breakout or volatility expansion; (c) sweep/reclaim or false-break/failed ignition; (d) bounded rotation/mean reversion; (e) persistence/runner lifecycle; (f) protective tail containment. A specialist comprises causal entry, hold, exit, and profit attribution contracts; not merely an indicator vote. Mechanisms may be simple if that suffices.
4. **Four coordinated functions.** ENTRY/ACTION chooses if, when and which side; HOLD/PERSISTENCE evaluates continuation/invalidation without observing future path; EXIT/HARVEST realizes or limits outcomes in tick order; PROFIT/ACCOUNT integrates opportunities into realistic net returns. STRUCTURAL OWNERSHIP, FAILED IGNITION, TAIL CONTAINMENT and OPPORTUNITY QUALITY are cross-cutting diagnostics, not extra independently additive accounts.
5. **Shared chronological portfolio arbitration.** Specialists contend for one account with defined priority, maximum concurrency, direction conflicts, position sizing, margin, costs, exposure, correlated-loss controls and stop-out rules. The system must prevent duplicate signals and incompatible simultaneous orders. Never sum standalone specialist profits to claim portfolio returns. The combined account is evaluated only by exact tick-order event replay with full cross-month continuation state.
6. **Complexity and redundancy control.** Every additional layer must have a distinct measurable responsibility, reconstructible public logic or a specified causal equation, and show incremental out-of-sample economic utility under ablation. Reject needless indicators, correlated filters, combinatorial parameter mining, opacity, and performance gains created only by deleting most trades.

## Falsification and evaluation protocol

- **Controls:** compare a causal simple single-specialist reference, each independent candidate, a state-aware router, incremental combinations, and the combined system *with the same data, costs, risk budget, and fair opportunity accounting*.
- **Ablations:** remove one component at a time and quantify changed entries, trades retained, gross profit, gross loss, realized net, worst-month loss, max drawdown, trade concentration, overlap, holding-time profile, and small-account survival. Distinguish entry quality from holding/exiting and portfolio routing.
- **Path specificity:** screening requires certified causal caches; shortlisted promising configurations require exact original Dukascopy tick replay. Complete Jan–Jul chronologically with monthly bounded/checkpointed evidence. Execution spans Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul are recovery groups, **not arbitrary discovery/OOS boundaries**. August remains SEALED. A market-state layer cannot be selected using its future outcome.
- **Risk and robustness:** commission, spread, slippage, parameter-neighborhood, abnormal volatility, worst-tail path, trade velocity and $100–$500 theoretical capital overlays. A high-frequency claim must survive realistic spreads and executable broker/minimum-lot constraints.
- **Promotion:** no owner-approved BETA EA exists; therefore no percentage can be manufactured versus an incumbent. A first baseline requires owner-approved comparator rules. Later candidates must satisfy preregistered *strictly greater than 10%* valid whole-system or category KPI improvement plus no unacceptable integrated regression; owner review **before** MQL5 code, then owner Coinexx M1 `Every tick based on real ticks` report and explicit disposition. `>=10%` is a notification threshold, not automatic approval.
- **R9 usage:** R9 REAL is a historical execution comparator; R9 SYNTH and OVERFIT show synthetic/hindsight capacity and missing components, **never** a tradable execution input or proof the capability transfers to Dukascopy/Coinexx. Quantify REAL→SYNTH reference distance with the cross-feed caveat and mark mathematically invalid bridges NOT_DEFINED.
- **Naming/publication:** earned formal breakthroughs only: `R09_BETA_<order>_<breakthrough-name>`, one transparent public white paper each, with complete Jan–Jul Net / Gross Loss / Max DD and proof. Failed/sub-10% studies remain reproducible and archived in Drive/GitHub without routine owner interruption.
